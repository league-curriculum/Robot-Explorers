title: "Session 7 — Maze Challenge"
---

**Opening concept:** Harder problems need more iteration, not more guessing.

**Activity:**
- Students build their own mazes for another team (constraints: at least 3 turns, must have a start and finish marker)
- Teams swap mazes and program Edison to navigate the new maze
- Scoring: fastest time to complete the maze successfully wins
- If a team finishes fast, they try their maze blindfolded (one person reads the program aloud, other navigates mentally)

**Engineering connection:** Designing a challenge for someone else requires understanding the problem deeply

**Materials:** Devices, USB cables, maze-building materials, timer
