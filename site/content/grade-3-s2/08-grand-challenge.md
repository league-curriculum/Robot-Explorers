title: "Session 8 — Grand Challenge"
---

**Opening concept:** Big challenges require combining everything you know.

**Activity:**
- Each team gets a "mission card" describing a course: e.g., "Drive to the ball, deliver it to the goal, then return to start and play a victory song"
- All programming done on devices, any EdBlocks features allowed
- 20 minutes to program, 10 minutes of runs, 10 minutes of debrief
- Debrief: what was the hardest part? What did you give up on and why?

**Engineering connection:** Real engineering involves tradeoffs and prioritization

**Materials:** Devices, USB cables, ball game setup, tape course, mission cards (teacher-prepared)
