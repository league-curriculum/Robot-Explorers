---

**Opening concept:** Real engineering problems don't come with instructions.

**Activity:**
- Run "Operation: Neko Prime" (scenario-based collaboration challenge from `curriculum/AdditionalActivities/Operation-Neko-Prime.pdf`)
- Teams receive a mission briefing and must coordinate their Edison programs to complete a scenario without step-by-step guidance
- Any programming level allowed: barcodes, Card Coding, EdBlocks, or EdScratch
- Debrief: how did your team make decisions when there was no clear right answer?

**Materials:** Operation: Neko Prime student sheets, all programming materials available
