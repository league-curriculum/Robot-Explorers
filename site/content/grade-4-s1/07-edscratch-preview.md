title: "Session 7 — EdScratch Preview / EdBlocks Capstone"
---

**Activity:**
- Students who have finished EdBlocks Activities 1–21: open EdScratch (edscratchapp.com), work through Unit 1 (Get Started) and the beginning of Unit 2 (Move It)
- Students still building comfort with EdBlocks: Activities 22–23 (light avoidance, dance party capstone)
- Both groups produce something to demo next session — either a Scratch-based program or the dance party performance
- Instructor circulates, noting who's ready for EdScratch in Class 6

**Materials:** Devices, USB cables, EdScratch student activity sheets (Units 1–2), EdBlocks student sheets 22–23
