title: "Session 2 — EdBlocks Express: Drive and Turn"
---

**Activity:**
- Fast-track for new students: Activities 8–11 in one session (EdBlocks environment, connect, drive, turn)
- Returning students: go straight to Activity 12 (maze) and build the most complex maze they can navigate
- Peer teaching: returning students help new students get connected and running
- At end: all students attempt the same 3-turn maze. Time it.

**Materials:** Devices, USB cables, pre-built 3-turn maze, timer
