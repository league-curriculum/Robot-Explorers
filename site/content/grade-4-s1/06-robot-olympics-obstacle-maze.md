title: "Session 6 — Robot Olympics, Event 2: Obstacle Maze Design"
---

**Opening concept:** Good engineers test their designs from the user's perspective.

**Activity:**
- Each team designs an obstacle maze for Event 2 (constraints: 4+ turns, at least one obstacle requiring sensor detection, must be completable)
- Teams must test their own maze first before handing it off
- Round-robin: each team programs Edison through one other team's maze. Time it.
- Championship: top 2 fastest teams go head-to-head on a combined "super maze"

**Materials:** Devices, USB cables, maze materials, timer
