title: "Session 1 — Robot Boot Camp (Grade 4 Edition)"
---

**Activity:**
- Ball game: two rounds, open to everyone, no prior experience needed
- Returning students who know EdBlocks: open challenge — program the ball game approach in EdBlocks as fast as possible
- New students: barcode exploration with a returning-student mentor; join EdBlocks challenge when ready
- Debrief: who got the fastest approach time? What did their program look like?

**Materials:** Ball game setup, barcode list, devices, USB cables
