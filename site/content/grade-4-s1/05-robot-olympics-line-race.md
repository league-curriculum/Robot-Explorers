title: "Session 5 — Robot Olympics, Event 1: Line Race"
---

**Opening concept:** Fair competition requires fair rules. Engineers set the rules.

**Activity:**
- Line-following race on a shared, teacher-built track. Tight curves, figure-8 shape, one section with a gap.
- Students program Edison to follow the line as fast as possible. Speed vs. accuracy tradeoff.
- Heats and finals. Record times.
- Engineering debrief: what parameter changes affected speed? What caused failures?

**Materials:** Devices, USB cables, large line-track mat (black tape on butcher paper), timer
