title: "Session 5 — Ball Game Tournament"
---

**Opening concept:** Improvement comes from trying, watching, and adjusting.

**Activity:**
- Round-robin tournament: 3-minute matches, count deliveries
- Between rounds, each team gets 2 minutes to practice and adjust their strategy
- Debrief: what changed between your first round and your last? Why?

**Engineering connection:** Full loop — Understand → Imagine → Build → Prove. Students articulate what they tried, what worked, what they changed.

**Materials:** Ball game setup, timer
